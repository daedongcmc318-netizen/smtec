CEO Message

CEO Message Philosophy & Goals History Certifications Organization Location

A company faithful to the fundamentals — committed to delivering satisfaction and trust to our customers.

Hello,
Thank you for visiting SM TECH Co., Ltd.

Founded in 1998, SM TECH has been continuously growing as a precision machining specialist in the defense, aerospace, and satellite industries.

Through the development of ultra-precision machining technology and special-shape processing methods, we have obtained patents for our machining processes. Our employees devote themselves wholeheartedly to becoming an ever-advancing company through endless challenges and innovation.

SM TECH operates under three management principles:
The finest technology and perfect quality
Challenge for the future and creation of opportunities
Action grounded in ethical conduct
We place customer value first and devote our full effort to delivering customer satisfaction through assured quality.

Building on our spirit of challenge, we focus all corporate capabilities on creating value through technological superiority and pursuing the highest standard at all times.

We will set our actions to the customer's perspective, fulfill our corporate ethics and social responsibility, and do our utmost to repay the support of our customers, communities, and all stakeholders.

We promise to be a company faithful to the fundamentals — delivering satisfaction and trust to our customers.

Thank you.

SM TECH Co., Ltd.
CEO   Shin Geun-seop
