Core Technology

Core Technology Excellence

We possess proprietary precision machining capabilities at the micron level required by the defense, aerospace, and satellite industries. Through 5-axis CNC machining and in-house metrology, we deliver uncompromising quality.

Quality First

Quality First Principle

We perform full inspection on every part using our 3D coordinate measuring machines, optical comparators, and height gauges. All parts are shipped with quality data attached.

Customer Trust

Customer Trust Realization

From design consultation to prototyping and mass production, we communicate consistently to respond promptly to customer requirements. We maintain long-term partnerships with major Korean defense and space companies including Hanwha Systems and Satrec Initiative.
